Utrecht

The Centraal Museum is the oldest municipal museum in the Netherlands. The museum is housed in a former medieval monastery at Agnietenstraat in Utrecht and shows art, design, fashion and city history.

Museum website

Location

Agnietenstraat 1, 3512 XA Utrecht

Map unavailable; use the address above.

Opening hours

Tuesday to Sunday 11 a.m.–5 p.m. Public holidays: King's Day (27 April) closed; Liberation Day (5 May) open; Easter Sunday and Easter Monday open; Ascension Day open; Whit Sunday and Whit Monday open; Christmas Day (25 December) closed; Boxing Day (26 December) open; New Year's Eve (31 December) open; New Year's Day (1 January) closed.

Getting there

Public transport

Bus 2 from Utrecht Central Station towards the Museum Quarter. Alternatively: train to Utrecht Vaartsche Rijn station, about a 10-minute walk. Up-to-date info: 9292.nl.

Parking

Car parks Vaartsche Rijn and Springweg, about a 10-minute walk. Utrecht P+R locations: day ticket €5, valid for up to 5 people (goedopweg.nl). Bicycle: racks next to the entrance; e-bike charging at Museumcafé Centraal.

Accessibility

Special wheelchair route (ask at the ticket desk or download the PDF). Some doors are difficult for wheelchair and walker users to open; a companion may be helpful. Free admission for companions (companion ticket only at the box office). Two wheelchair-accessible toilets. Mobility scooters up to 120 x 70 x 109 cm allowed (in consultation). Wheelchairs, walker and stools available to borrow (reserve: info@centraalmuseum.nl). Service dogs welcome. Disabled parking spaces at Nicolaaskerkhof (2).

Entrance cards

  • ICOM — Accepted (Free admission.)
  • Rembrandtkaart — Accepted (Free admission.)
  • U-Pas — Accepted (Free admission (U-Pas).)

Prices

Day tickets: €18 adults; €10.50 CJP/student card; €10 ages 13-17; free for 0-12, Museumkaart, De Vriendenloterij VIPKAART, Vriendenpas, ICOM, Rembrandt card, U-Pas.
