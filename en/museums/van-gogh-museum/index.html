Van Gogh Museum

Back to all museums

Amsterdam

The Van Gogh Museum in Amsterdam is the museum about Vincent van Gogh. Two buildings – the Rietveld Building and the Kurokawa Wing – together form the Van Gogh Museum. The museum first opened its doors in 1973 and has been inspiring people around the world with the artworks of Vincent van Gogh and his contemporaries for over 50 years.

Opening hours

Open daily from 9:00; closing time varies by period (full day-by-day schedule on the site). Until end of September: 9:00–18:00, Fridays until 21:00 (except 29 May, 31 July and 25 September; 'Vincent on Friday'). October–December: 9:00–17:00 or 9:00–18:00 depending on the date; Friday evenings often until 21:00 (except 27 November). Holidays: 24, 25, 26 and 31 December 9:00–17:00; 19–23 and 27–30 December until 18:00.

Getting there

Public transport

Nearest stops: Museumplein (tram 2, 5 or 24; bus 357 or 397) or Concertgebouw (tram 5, 12 or 24; bus 347 or 357). Tram 12 (direction Frederik Hendrik Plantsoen): get off at Concertgebouw stop. Tram 19: change at Leidseplein to tram 2, 5 or 24; get off at Museumplein stop. Plan your journey via the 9292 website or app, or call +31 900 9292.

Parking

Located underneath Museumplein, Q-Park (entrance Van Baerlestraat) is the closest car park to the museum. Paid parking applies on the streets near the museum. Several disabled parking spaces near Museumplein.

Accessibility

Accessible to everyone, with or without a (hidden) disability. Visitors who are unable to navigate the museum independently may bring a companion free of charge; mobility aids or a wheelchair can be borrowed. Assistance dogs allowed; visitors with sensory sensitivity can wear the Hidden Disabilities Sunflower Keychain. Provisions include: accessible lifts, barrier-free building, wheelchair-friendly restroom, hearing loop and audio description.

Prices

Adults € 25; visitors under 18 free. Students € 16 (only for those enrolled at an educational institution within the EU/EEA); CJP € 11. Free: Museumkaart, Vincent's Friends, VriendenLoterij VIP-KAART, ICOM card, Rembrandt card, Stadspas. No exhibition fees; tickets are online only, with a start time.
