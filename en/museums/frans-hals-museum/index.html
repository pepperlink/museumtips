Frans Hals Museum

Back to all museums

Haarlem

The Frans Hals Museum is Haarlem's museum of fine arts. It holds the largest collection of Frans Hals paintings in the world, alongside works by other famous Haarlem artists such as Judith Leyster, Cornelis van Haarlem and Jacob van Ruisdael. The collection also includes a rich range of art from 1880 to the present (including Charley Toorop and Dana Lixenberg). The museum shows 16th- and 17th-century, modern and contemporary art in Haarlem's historic city centre.

Location

Groot Heiligland 62, 2011 ES Haarlem

Opening hours

Tuesday to Sunday: 11 am - 5 pm During the Hals-Rembrandt exhibition the museum opens as early as 10.00 am. Extra open on: Easter Monday, Whit Monday and 28 December. The museum closes at 4 pm on 24 and 31 December. Closed on: New Year's Day (1 January), King's Day (27 April) and Christmas Day (25 December).

Getting there

Public transport

The Frans Hals Museum is about a 20-minute walk from Haarlem train station (via the Grote Markt and the Grote Houtstraat; the second street on the right is the Groot Heiligland). By bus: the bus station is on the south side of the station; nearby stops include Frans Hals Museum (line 3, 1-minute walk), Centrum Houtplein (several lines, 6 minutes), Turfmarkt (several lines, 6 minutes) and Centrum/Verwulft (8 minutes). Plan your trip via the public transport journey planner or call +31 (0)900 9292.

Parking

The Houtplein and De Kamp car parks are both a five-minute walk from the museum. With a disabled parking permit (blue badge), cars may park free of charge throughout Haarlem in general disabled parking spaces and general parking bays; the permit is not valid in multi-storey car parks. The city also has 2 disabled parking spaces in De Kamp car park and 11 in Houtplein car park (each a 350-metre walk).

Accessibility

The museum is largely step-free and largely accessible to wheelchairs, mobility scooters and walking bikes; the room with the 18th-century doll's house is not accessible to wheelchair users, and the regents' and regentesses' rooms are reached via two steps with a handrail. Facilities: assistance dogs permitted, lightweight portable stools, wheelchair-friendly toilet in the middle of the museum near the militia room, baby changing facilities, breastfeeding room, free cloakroom and lockers, free Wi-Fi, audio tour and paper map. Carers accompanying visitors with disabilities receive free admission; staff are familiar with the Sunflower Keycord. Wheelchairs can be reserved via info@franshalsmuseum.nl or 023 511 57 75.
