Kröller-Müller Museum

Back to all museums

Otterlo

The Kröller-Müller Museum is located in the heart of De Hoge Veluwe National Park, near Otterlo. Since the museum opened in 1938, Vincent van Gogh's artworks have been on display at the heart of the building, the Van Gogh Gallery, and they hold a special place in the collection of Anton and Helene Kröller-Müller, the founders of the museum. The sculpture garden features over two hundred modern sculptures by artists from around the world.

Opening hours

Museum and sculpture garden: Tuesday to Sunday 10.00–17.00 hrs; public holidays 10.00–17.00 hrs; closed on 1 January. Monday open only in the summer period (6 July to 24 August 2026); every day open from 10.00 to 17.00 hrs during that period. Public holidays: Easter, Ascension Day, Pentecost, Christmas 25 and 26 December, King's Day 27 April, Liberation Day 5 May. Please note: on Saturday 19 September 2026 the Van Gogh galleries close at 14.30 (official opening); other galleries and the sculpture garden remain open until 17.00.

Getting there

Public transport

Bus from the railway stations of Apeldoorn, Ede-Wageningen or Arnhem to Otterlo; there you transfer to the bus for the museum (this bus stops at the park entrance; show or buy your park ticket there). From Ede-Wageningen: in summer (Sat and Sun, 28 June to 27 September) bus 106 runs directly to the museum; Mon–Fri: bus 108 (towards Apeldoorn) → Otterlo, Rotonde stop, then bus 106 (towards De Hoge Veluwe National Park) → Kröller-Müller Museum stop. From Arnhem Centraal: bus 105 (towards Barneveld) → Otterlo, Rotonde stop, then bus 106 → Kröller-Müller Museum stop.

Parking

Park at one of the three park entrances (Otterlo 2.5 km; Hoenderloo 4 km; Schaarsbergen 10 km to the museum) or at the park pavilion near the Museonder. Continue by white bike (free, all white bikes have a child seat) or on foot. Travel times (car/bike/on foot): Otterlo 5/10/40 min; Hoenderloo 10/25/90 min; Schaarsbergen 15/40/over 150 min; Museonder 3/5/15 min.

Accessibility

Easily accessible by wheelchair or mobility scooter. Wheelchairs, walking frames, crutches and children's buggies available for loan free of charge from the cloakroom. Sculpture garden: paths asphalted at the entrance, partly paved/unpaved further back (marked on the map); in summer wheelchairs are welcome on the lawn; segways permitted, other motorised (electric) mobility aids are not. Assistance dogs are welcome in the museum, the sculpture garden and the park. Six accessible parking spaces 175 metres from the entrance; with a disabled parking card, free museum entry for one companion (the companion pays the normal park admission).
