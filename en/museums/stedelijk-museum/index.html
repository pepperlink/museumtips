Back to all museums

Amsterdam

The Stedelijk Museum Amsterdam is the place where everyone can discover and experience modern and contemporary visual art and design, 365 days a year. The permanent collection presentation features around 500 artworks and design objects dating from 1870 to the present day, alongside changing exhibitions. The museum is located on Museumplein in Amsterdam.

Getting there

Public transport

From Amsterdam Central Station: tram 2 or 12, stop Museumplein; metro 52 to De Pijp station, change to tram 3 or 12. From Amsterdam Amstel: tram 12, stop Museumplein. From Amsterdam Zuid WTC: tram 5, stop Museumplein (sometimes announced as Paulus Potterstraat). From Sloterdijk: tram 19 to Leidseplein, change; from Muiderpoort: tram 3, stop Museumplein.

Parking

Q-Park garage under Museumplein (entrance opposite the Concertgebouw); paid parking around Museumplein. Disability accessible parking at the group entrance, Paulus Potterstraat 13; several disabled parking spaces in the Q-Park garage. With a European disabled parking permit, parking is free in almost all places in Amsterdam.

Accessibility

The museum has elevators, wheelchair elevators and wheelchair-accessible restrooms; elevators can be used independently (see floor plan). Wheelchairs, walkers, folding chairs and Alinkers can be borrowed free of charge at the information desk (reserve via reserveringen@stedelijk.nl or 020 5732 911). Assistance dogs are welcome; care-givers have free admission; a hearing loop and sign-language provisions are available. When the museum is busy, assistive devices may already be allocated – reserve in advance.

Entrance cards

  • Museumkaart — Accepted (Free; booking a free ticket/time slot is possible and recommended (required for exhibitions with a start time).)
  • Vriendenloterij — Accepted (VriendenLoterij VIP-KAART: free.)
  • ICOM — Accepted (Free.)
  • Stadspas — Accepted (Free.)
  • I amsterdam City Card — Accepted (Free.)
  • Rembrandtkaart — Accepted (Free.)

Prices

Adults € 22.50; students and CJP € 12.50; under 19 free; Stedelijk Members (including friends) free. Free with Museumkaart, VriendenLoterij VIP-KAART, I amsterdam City Card, Stadspas, ICOM, Rembrandtkaart, CIMAM, AICA and companion of a visitor with a disability. A surcharge applies to some exhibitions (once only; voucher/sticker for repeat visits; Members never pay a surcharge). Tickets online only.
