Museum Kranenburgh

Back to all museums

Bergen NH

Museum Kranenburgh is a museum for modern and contemporary art in the green artist village of Bergen. Many artists, from around 1900 to the present, feel attracted and inspired by the landscape of the polder, the dunes, the forest and the beach in and around Bergen. The museum maintains an extensive collection of art from 1900 to the present, the core of which is the art of the Bergen School.

Getting there

Public transport

From Alkmaar station, take bus line 166 to Bergen, stop Plein.

Parking

Limited parking at the museum; municipal parking lot across from the museum, up to 2 hours with a parking disc. During events: Parkeergarage Centrum Bergen on the Plein (about a 7-minute walk). No space for buses in the museum car park; coaches can park briefly in the municipal car park opposite and for longer periods in the VV Bergen car park on Groeneweg.

Accessibility

Easily accessible for people with physical disabilities, with disabled parking spaces, elevators and a wheelchair-friendly toilet. Two parking spaces for disabled visitors right next to the museum entrance (call for assistance: 072 2015000). Easily accessible with wheelchairs, walkers, mobility scooters and strollers; two wheelchairs and one walker available for loan (reservations: 072 2015000). Free admission for companions. Accessible with an assistance dog, provided it is kept on a leash.

Prices

Adults €16.50; children up to 16 free; students/CJP card €6.50; combination ticket Museum Kranenburgh and Het Sterkenhuis €21.00. Free: Museum card, ICOM card, Friends Kranenburgh, Rembrandtpas. Please note: Museum Kranenburgh is a debit-card-only museum: all payments (counter, museum café and museum shop) by debit card. Tickets are non-refundable.
