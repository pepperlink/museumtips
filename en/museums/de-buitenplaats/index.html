Eelde

Drents Museum De Buitenplaats in Eelde focuses on organic architecture, figurative art and art from around 1900. The museum pavilion was founded in the early 1990s by collectors Jos and Janneke van Groeningen and opened by Queen Beatrix in 1996; the organic design is by architectural firm Alberts & Van Huut. The site includes the 17th-century Nijsinghhuis with wall and ceiling paintings by northern figurative artists and a freely accessible museum garden. Exhibitions change twice a year with a focus on art nouveau.

Location

Opening hours

Tue–Sun 11 am–5 pm (museum building and Nijsinghhuis). Museum garden and Brasserie Röling: Tue–Sun 10 am–5 pm, free entry. Also open on Mondays during school holidays. Extra open: summer-holiday Mondays 4 Jul–30 Aug, Mon 12 & 19 Oct, Mon 21 & 28 Dec. Closed: 25 December (Christmas Day) and 1 January (New Year's Day).

Getting there

Public transport

Bus 9 from Groningen Central Station towards Eelde (twice an hour); stop Kerkhoflaan or Kosterijweg. Bus 50 towards Assen via De Punt with a transfer to the Eelde bus (stop Kosterijweg or Kerkhoflaan). From Assen station: bus 50 towards De Punt, transfer to the Eelde bus.

Parking

Free parking next to the museum on the market square (opposite Grand Café OER) or at the large Jumbo car park. Bicycle parking at the museum entrance.

Accessibility

Museum and Brasserie Röling easily accessible with wheelchair, walker, mobility scooter (smaller models) and pram or buggy. Nijsinghhuis has limited accessibility: ground floor via the front door at Hoofdweg 80 (ring the bell); upper floor not accessible; not accessible with a mobility scooter. Museum garden limited accessibility (thresholds and steps). Wheelchair and walker available for loan (reserve in advance); assistance dogs welcome. Disabled toilet and baby-changing facility in the museum building.
