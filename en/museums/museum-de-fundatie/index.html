Museum de Fundatie

Back to all museums

Zwolle

Museum de Fundatie is one of the leading museums in the east of the Netherlands. The museum brings a rich history together with the world of today and manages two collections: the collection of Dirk Hannema and the collection of the Province of Overijssel. Its main location is in the centre of Zwolle; the second location is Kasteel het Nijenhuis in Heino.

Opening hours

Closed on Mondays; Tuesday to Sunday 11:00–17:00. Exception: Zwolle location closed from 21 to 25 September 2026 due to renovation; on 26 and 27 September only the Fundatie Collection is visible, free admission. Silent Sunday: last Sunday of the month, 10:00–11:00. Fundatie Friday: first Friday of the month, 17:00–21:00.

Getting there

Public transport

Train: Zwolle railway station, about a 12-minute walk to the museum.

Parking

Four parking facilities within walking distance (near the A28 exit): Maagjesbolwerk (350 m, approx. 5 min), Burg. Van Rooijensingel (450 m, approx. 6 min), Emmawijk (550 m, approx. 7 min), Katwolderplein (600 m, approx. 8 min). Please note: parking at the museum (Potgietersingel) is limited to a maximum of 60 minutes. Disabled parking spaces: Potgietersingel (2) and Blijmarkt (2).

Accessibility

Easily accessible with a manual or electric wheelchair and with a walker; partly accessible with a mobility scooter, depending on its size (may vary per exhibition). Step-free entrance at the rear of the museum (Potgietersingel). Wheelchair-accessible toilet with support rails. Wheelchairs available to borrow (reserve: +31 572 388188); gallery stools available at the desk. Free admission for companions; officially trained assistance dogs are welcome. Disabled parking spaces: Potgietersingel (2) and Blijmarkt (2).

Entrance cards

  • Museumkaart — Accepted (Free admission; Museumkaart for sale at the ticket desk (€75, ages 19+).)
  • Vriendenloterij — Accepted (VriendenLoterij VIP card: free admission.)
  • Rembrandtkaart — Accepted (Rembrandtkaart (Vereniging Rembrandt): free admission.)

Prices

Adults €17,50; CJP cardholders and students €7,50; groups of 10 or more €13,50 p.p.; young people aged 18 and under free. Free: Museumkaart, VriendenLoterij VIP card, Friends of Museum de Fundatie, Rembrandtkaart, ArtEZ Artisteducator en Art & Design. Combination visit Zwolle & Heino (both within 2 days): €25.00. No VVV gift vouchers or museum gift cards; Museumkaart €75 (ages 19+).
