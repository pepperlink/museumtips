Museum Volkenkunde

Back to all museums

Leiden

Museum Volkenkunde is now called Wereldmuseum Leiden and is part of the Wereldmuseum. With its scientific orientation and collecting history, it focuses on global cultural histories, with themes including icons of the world and the beauty of indigenous worlds and their struggle for independence and the restoration of historical injustice. The exhibitions invite visitors to reflect on what it means to be human.

Opening hours

Tue–Sun 10 am–5 pm. Also open on Mondays during school holidays and on various public holidays. Extra open on Mondays (2026): 16 & 23 Feb, 6 Apr (Easter), 20 Apr, 25 May (Pentecost), 6/13/20/27 Jul and 3/10/17/24 Aug, 12 & 19 Oct, 21 & 28 Dec. Getting there

Public transport

Train: five minutes' walk from Leiden Centraal railway station.

Parking

Paid parking nearby; nearest garage: Morspoortgarage, within walking distance. Also the Haagweg car park, with a free shuttle bus to the museum. Two disabled parking spaces at the main entrance (free with a disability card).

Accessibility

Easily accessible for wheelchair users: barrier-free building, lift to all floors, wheelchair-friendly toilet; some push wheelchairs available. Not accessible with a mobility scooter or bicycle trailer. Assistance dogs and guide dogs allowed (on a leash). One companion free with a Visitor's Ticket. Guided tour for blind and partially sighted visitors; sensory map available.

Entrance cards

  • Museumkaart — Accepted (Free entry; show a physical card or the official Museumkaart app. Museumkaart can be bought at the ticket desk.)
  • VriendenLoterij — Accepted (VriendenLoterij VIP card: free entry.)
  • ICOM — Accepted (ICOM: free entry.)
  • Vereniging Rembrandt — Accepted (Vereniging Rembrandt: free entry.)

Prices

2026 admission: adults € 16 online / € 18 at the ticket desk. Children 6–18: € 6.50 online / € 8 at the ticket desk; 0–5 free. CJP from 19: € 10; CJP up to 18: € 7; students € 11; Leiden University students free. Free: Museumkaart, VriendenLoterij VIP card, ICOM, Vereniging Rembrandt and companion of a disabled visitor.
