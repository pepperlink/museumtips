Wassenaar

Museum Voorlinden is a museum for modern and contemporary art on the Voorlinden estate in Wassenaar. The museum connects people, art, nature and architecture; its building by Kraaijvanger Architects is in every respect subservient to the art. The museum collects, presents, keeps, manages, conserves, restores and publishes modern and contemporary art, both on its own premises and elsewhere. Voorlinden aims to be an oasis of tranquillity where people can marvel and be surprised.

Museum website

Location

Buurtweg 90, 2244 AG Wassenaar

Opening hours

Open every day, 7 days a week and 365 days a year: 11 am - 5 pm. The museum is also open during public holidays.

Getting there

Public transport

Bus 43 or 44 from The Hague Central or Leiden Central train station to the Wittenburgerweg stop in Wassenaar. From the bus stop it is about a 20-minute walk to Voorlinden (route marked with signs). OV-bike rental available; from The Hague Central and Laan van NOI approx. 25 minutes by bike.

Parking

Free parking on the estate in two car parks: - P1: closest to the museum and restaurant, just before the barrier; reserved parking spaces for disabled visitors; approx. 300 m walk. - P2: immediately on your left when driving up the estate; for coaches and for picking up/dropping off (coaches cannot turn around in P1). Nearest charging station: car park by the sports complex on the Buurtweg.

Accessibility

Museum Voorlinden is easily accessible for people using a wheelchair, walker, buggy or mobility scooter; the building is mainly on one level and has a special entrance of max. 90 cm wide. A wheelchair can be borrowed free of charge (reserve via info@voorlinden.nl at least two working days ahead; € 50 cash deposit). Up to 1 assistant free of charge. Registered guide dogs welcome. Hearing teleloop system in the auditorium. Disabled parking spaces on P1; adapted toilet in the museum. Silent hour ('Stilte op sokken'): every Saturday and Sunday morning 10 - 11 am (limited number of visitors). Please note: a few artworks are of limited accessibility (e.g. Swimming Pool can only be viewed from above with a wheelchair; Open Ended has corners too narrow for most wheelchairs).
