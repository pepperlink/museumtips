Bonnefantenmuseum

Back to all museums

Maastricht

The Bonnefanten is the art museum of Limburg, in Maastricht. It presents permanent collections and changing exhibitions with non-mainstream art and artists. The museum dedicates a permanent tribute to architect Aldo Rossi, the architect of the Bonnefanten.

Getting there

Public transport

From Maastricht Central Station: bus line 1 towards De Heeg, line 5 towards Heugem, line 57 towards Gulpen, line 78 (IC) to Liège and line 350 towards Aachen Centrum. Get off at the 'Bonnefantenmuseum' stop.

Parking

The museum's own car park (access via 'Daemslunet') has one disabled parking space and is often full; you can always ask whether there is space. Parking garages within walking distance: Q-Park Plein 1992 (Plein 1992 53, 6221 JP; 6 adapted spaces; maximum height 1.90 m; ± 10 minutes' walk) and Q-Park Bonnefantenmuseum (Serpentilunet 1, 6221 KW; maximum height 1.90 m; note: no lift, only a staircase - not suitable when using a wheelchair). There is also paid street parking on the Avenue Céramique.

Accessibility

The main entrance can be reached by wheelchair, rollator, pram or mobility scooter via a ramp at the front of the museum building. The museum is largely accessible for wheelchairs; all floors are accessible by passenger lift (H: 230 - L: 140 - W: 160), with benches and portable museum stools on every floor. If it is not possible to use the regular lift with a mobility aid, the goods lift can exceptionally be used (not to the third floor). There are two adapted toilets on the ground floor (next to the lift and next to the cloakroom), with baby changing facilities. Visitors who need assistance receive free admission for their companion. Assistance dogs, with a harness and leash, are welcome on production of the relevant pass. Reserve a wheelchair via +31 (0)43 329 0111 or info@bonnefanten.nl. Note: the third floor is only accessible via the lift and the staircase on the south side; exhibitions on the third floor have limited access for wheelchair users.
