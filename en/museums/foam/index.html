Foam

Amsterdam

Foam is an international and multifaceted photography museum on the Keizersgracht in Amsterdam, housed in a historic canalside building. The museum presents up to four exhibitions at a time, ranging from documentary to fashion and from contemporary to historic photography, and publishes Foam Magazine. Foam also organises debates, publications, workshops and educational programmes around photography.

Museum website

Location

Keizersgracht 609, 1017 DS Amsterdam

Opening hours

Daily 10.00–18.00; Thursday and Friday until 21.00. Please note: Foam is temporarily closed from 27 August until 17 September 2026 for sustainability works; reopening Friday 18 September 2026 at 10.00.

Getting there

Public transport

Foam states it is best reached on foot or by public transport; plan your route with the public transport journey planner (9292) or Google Maps. Address: Keizersgracht 609, Amsterdam.

Parking

The website does not list parking information; there is a loading zone where visitors can be dropped off.

Accessibility

The historic building is not fully wheelchair accessible: not all rooms are reachable by lift, and mobility scooters/electric wheelchairs are not permitted. There is an elevator, a wheelchair-accessible bathroom and a drop-off zone; folding stools are available at the front desk. Companions of visitors with disabilities have free admission; guide dogs are allowed (leashed and registered). Please contact the museum before your visit to discuss the possibilities.
