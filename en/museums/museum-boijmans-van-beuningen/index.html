Museum Boijmans Van Beuningen (Depot)

Back to all museums

Rotterdam

The Depot of Museum Boijmans Van Beuningen in Rotterdam's Museumpark is the world's first publicly accessible art storage facility; the MVRDV-designed building opened on 5 November 2021. Fourteen climate-controlled compartments house the museum's complete collection of more than 155,000 objects, amassed since 1849. Visitors get a behind-the-scenes look at museum work, from conservation studios to storage and transport. The museum itself is temporarily closed for a large-scale renovation; the Depot remains open.

Getting there

Public transport

Metro lines A, B or C, stop Eendrachtsplein (approx. 350 m walk); tram, stop Museumpark (approx. 290 m walk). Plan your route via 0900-9292 or the planner on the website.

Parking

The Museumpark parking garage is located under the Depot (entrance via Museumpark 32, 3015 CX Rotterdam); capacity is limited due to construction work. Buses can park at Willemsplein. The Depot can only be reached via Wytemaweg.

Accessibility

The Depot is fully accessible for visitors with walking difficulties and visitors using a walker, rollator or (electric) wheelchair; all floors are accessible by lift, and very large wheelchairs can use the art lift on request (contact onderwijs@boijmans.nl in advance). The entrance is a revolving door; larger wheelchairs and assistance/guide dogs can use the group entrance on the other side of the building. Visitors who cannot move through the depot independently may bring a companion free of charge with a public transport companion card (the companion needs no ticket or time slot). Assistance dogs are welcome. No thresholds, wide doors, braille lift buttons, wheelchair-accessible toilets on the ground and sixth floors; there are no adult changing tables. Please note: the building's open character with glass balustrades may cause vertigo; walking poles are not allowed, sticks/crutches are available to borrow.

Prices

Adults €20; up to age 18 free; students/CJP €10.50; Friends of Boijmans free. Museumkaart, Rotterdampas, ICOM, Vereniging Rembrandt and VriendenLoterij VIP-KAART: €0 (book a time slot online). €2.50 surcharge during the Marisol exhibition (9 October 2026 – 29 March 2027); no surcharge for children up to 18, Friends of Boijmans, Rotterdampas, ICOM/CIMAM/AICA/IAA, Rembrandt card or Stichting Vluchtelingenwerk. Access is time-slot based; tickets online and at the Depot entrance. The Depot is cashless (card or credit card only).
