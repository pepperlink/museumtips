Fries Museum

Back to all museums

Leeuwarden

The Fries Museum in Leeuwarden manages the largest regional collection in the Netherlands, with more than 200,000 objects. The permanent exhibition Ferhaal fan Fryslân presents Frisian art and history, including works by Sir Lawrence Alma-Tadema, Gerrit Benner and Wybrand de Geest and stories of Grutte Pier, Mata Hari and the Vikings. The Fries Verzetsmuseum on the second floor covers Friesland during the Second World War. Children and young people up to 18 enter free of charge.

Museum website

Location

Wilhelminaplein 92, 8911 BS Leeuwarden

Opening hours

Tue–Sun 11 am–5 pm. Also open: Easter Sunday and Monday, Pentecost and Boxing Day. Closed: New Year's Day, King's Day (27 April) and Christmas Day. Café Thús: closed on Mondays, Tue–Sun from 10.30 am.

Getting there

Public transport

Train/bus: a 5-minute walk from Leeuwarden train and bus station.

Parking

Zaailand parking garage below the museum (24/7; direct entrance into the museum hall; several disabled spaces; maximum € 10 per day). If full: Oldehove, De Klanderij or Hoeksterend garages. Bicycle: free parking at Zaailand.

Accessibility

Modern building, easily accessible for the disabled; all floors reachable by lift; a disabled toilet on each floor. Wheelchairs and small mobility scooters available free of charge (reserve via infobalie@friesmuseum.nl). Free guide for visitors who cannot visit the museum on their own; guide dogs welcome. Sign-language interpreter available on request; sign-language tours and a special dementia tour available. Low-stimulus visits on request (doors open one hour earlier).

Entrance cards

  • Museumkaart — Accepted (Museumkaart: free entry.)
  • VriendenLoterij — Accepted (Vriendenloterij VIP card: free; admits one guest free of charge.)
  • Vereniging Rembrandt — Accepted (Rembrandtkaart (Vereniging Rembrandt): free entry.)

Prices

Adults € 17.50; children up to 17 free; students/CJP/ISIC € 8; groups (min. 10) € 14 p.p. Free: Museumkaart, Rembrandtkaart, Friends of the Fries Museum, Vriendenloterij VIP card, companions of wheelchair users. The Vriendenloterij VIP card admits one guest free of charge. Reservation not required.
