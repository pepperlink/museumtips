Huis Marseille

Back to all museums

Amsterdam

Huis Marseille has been Amsterdam's first photography museum since September 1999, located at Keizersgracht 401. The museum presents a rich and varied exhibition programme in two 17th-century canal houses, with around fourteen exhibition spaces (expanded with Keizersgracht 399 in 2013). Exhibitions change about three times a year; besides its own collection of modern photography, the museum has a library, a specialised photo book shop and a canal garden.

Opening hours

Daily 10:00–18:00; Thursdays 10:00–21:00. Closed: 27 April (King's Day), 25 December, 1 January, and during the construction of new exhibitions (in the week preceding the opening). On 31 December the museum is open from 10:00 to 17:00. Open on all other public holidays, including Easter Monday.

Getting there

Public transport

From Amsterdam Central Station: tram 2, 17 or 24, get off at Keizersgracht stop and walk 4 minutes. From Amsterdam Zuid Station: metro 52 (North-South line) towards Station Noord, get off at Rokin stop and walk 8 minutes. Huis Marseille is best reached by bicycle or public transport.

Parking

Park in Q-Park Europarking (Marnixstraat) or in parking garage Kalverstraat (Singel).

Accessibility

Due to the historic character and listed status of the two canal houses, the museum is unfortunately not fully accessible; because of the many staircases the galleries are particularly difficult for wheelchair and walker users. A pram can be left at reception during the visit. If you need an escort to visit the museum, they are granted free admission. Registered assistance dogs are allowed (with a vest/harness and the handler's identification card); other pets are not. Gallery texts are available in larger print on request.

Prices

Admission € 12.50. € 10.00 for groups of 8 or more (notify at least one week in advance via educatie@huismarseille.nl). € 6.50: students, senior citizens (65+), CJP, Rembrandtpas, Uitpas. Free: children ages 17 and under, Museumkaart, I amsterdam City Card, Stadspas, ICOM. Tickets can be purchased at the box office; an entrance ticket provides access to the exhibition(s), the library, the garden and garden house.
