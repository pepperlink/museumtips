Laren

Singer Laren is a unique private piece of cultural heritage in Laren, consisting of a museum, the villa 'De Wilde Zwanen', a theatre and a sculpture garden. American couple Anna and William Singer built their villa in the artists' village of Laren in the early twentieth century and amassed an impressive art collection. After William's death, Anna had a museum and theatre built onto the villa, opened to the public in 1956. A new theatre opened in 2017, followed by a museum wing for the Nardinc Collection in 2021.

Getting there

Public transport

By train to NS station Hilversum, Naarden-Bussum or Bussum-Zuid; continue by bus or OV-bike. Bus 109 from Naarden-Bussum station (direction Hilversum): stop Singermuseum, right in front of the museum; approx. 20 min, once an hour, 7 days a week. Bus 108 from Hilversum station (direction Huizen): stop Brink, then approx. 5 min walk; total travel time approx. 17 min. R-net line 321 from Amsterdam Zuid station to stop Laren, Singermuseum; approx. 45 min, every 30 min on weekdays. OV-bike: approx. 15-20 min cycling from the NS stations.

Parking

Free parking in the large car park on the Hein Keverweg, directly next to Singer Laren; blue zone with max. 4 hours parking between 09.00 - 18.00 (Mon-Sun) - blue parking disc required. When busy: other free parking options in the municipality of Laren; on Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays 50 extra free spaces on part of the Theodotion site (entrance Drift). Five disabled parking spaces directly next to Singer Laren. Six charging points for electric cars at the Hein Keverweg; e-bike charging point at the bicycle shed (daily 9 am - 6 pm, 4 bikes). Request: please do not park in the adjacent streets.

Accessibility

Singer Laren is fully wheelchair accessible, including the garden. Wheelchairs and rollators available on reservation (wheelchair via beveiliging@singerlaren.nl). One free companion for visitors unable to move through the museum independently; assistance dogs welcome. Disabled parking spaces close to the entrance; wheelchair-accessible toilet opposite the cloakroom (key at the desk). Large-print books available at the information desk; audiotours via the Bloomberg Connects app (with transcript, Knisper audio for the hard of hearing). Low-stimulus openings per exhibition (limited visitor numbers, reduced stimuli); quieter moments on weekdays after 3 pm. Accessibility questions: svalkenier@singerlaren.nl.
