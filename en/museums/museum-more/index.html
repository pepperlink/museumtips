Gorssel

Museum MORE is the leading museum for modern realist art in the Netherlands, with locations in Gorssel and Kasteel Ruurlo, and from September 2026 also Twickel in Delden. The collection focuses on modern realist art from 1900 to the present by artists with Dutch nationality and makers who have been active in the Netherlands. The Gorssel museum is housed in and behind the former town hall from 1914, extended with a pavilion-like new wing by museum architect Hans van Heeswijk.

Opening hours

MORE Gorssel: Tuesday to Sunday 10.00–17.00; closed on Mondays (open on some holiday Mondays; see the adjusted opening hours on the website). Closed on King's Day (27 April), Christmas Day (25 December) and New Year's Day (1 January). MORE Ruurlo and MORE Twickel: Tuesday to Sunday 11.00–17.00.

Getting there

Public transport

Travel to NS station Zutphen or NS station Deventer and take Arriva bus line 81; get off after about 15 minutes at the Museum MORE bus stop in Gorssel. At weekends, Arriva bus line 450 also runs from Zutphen station to Gorssel (and back).

Parking

Parking is free on the museum grounds at the rear of the building; there are several disabled parking spaces next to the museum.

Accessibility

MORE Gorssel is well accessible for visitors with disabilities and wheelchair users: free loan wheelchairs are available (reserve up to three days in advance via the form on the website), and there is a lift and a wheelchair-accessible toilet with grab rails. To the right of the main entrance (a revolving door) there is a special entrance door suitable for wheelchair users. The museum café can be reached via a ramp as well as stairs. Assistance dogs wearing a harness are welcome. Museum MORE Gorssel holds the green quality mark of Stichting Uit met Autisme (an autism-friendly venue).
