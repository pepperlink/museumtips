Den Haag

Fotomuseum Den Haag is dedicated to photography as an art form and offers visitors space for quiet reflection on the layers, meanings and context of photography. It sits next to Kunstmuseum Den Haag and shares its building with KM21. The Kunstmuseum's photography collection grew so substantially that a separate museum was founded in 2002, since expanded to show several exhibitions simultaneously.

Getting there

Public transport

Bus 24 and tram 17 from The Hague Central Station run directly to the museum; tram 17 also from Hollands Spoor Station. Tram stop approx. 50 m from the main entrance; bus stop approx. 215 m. Tram 17 and bus 24 have disabled access; mobility scooters and electric wheelchairs cannot be taken on HTM buses.

Parking

You can park your car around the museum; paid parking applies from 1 pm. The neighbouring Kunstmuseum has two disabled parking spaces (free with a disabled parking card), approx. 100 m from the Fotomuseum entrance; if occupied, only street parking remains. There are no indoor car parks with adapted spaces nearby.

Accessibility

The museum notes it does not have good disabled access throughout the building. Entrance via a ramp (no resting places, gradient 5.2% - 8.7%); call 070 - 338 12 197 for assistance. Outer door 87 cm, inner door 78 cm (can be opened wider). Adapted toilet on level -1 (via lift); central lift to all levels, plus a lift at the back of the galleries. Limited seating; no portable chairs. Questions: info@fotomuseumdenhaag.nl.
