Amsterdam

The Rijksmuseum is the national museum of the Netherlands, located on Museumplein in Amsterdam. Its collection spans more than 8,000 works covering 800 years of Dutch art and history, including masterpieces such as Rembrandt's The Night Watch and Vermeer's The Milkmaid. The museum is housed in a historic building, surrounded by gardens that are free to visit.

Location

Opening hours

Daily 9:00–17:00, including all public holidays. Shop and The Café: daily 9:00–18:00 (no ticket needed after 17:00). Rijksmuseum Gardens: 9:00–18:00, open in summer only.

Getting there

Public transport

Several tram lines stop close to the museum, from Amsterdam Zuid, Central Station and Amsterdam Amstel. Metro 52 to Vijzelgracht station; less than a 10-minute walk from there. Plan your journey via the public transport travel information service or +31 900 9292.

Parking

Paid parking nearby; Q-Park Museumplein garage (Van Baerlestraat 33b) under Museumplein. Several disabled parking spaces in the vicinity of the museum. P+R ArenA and P+R Zeeburg: park on the city edge and continue by public transport.

Accessibility

Wheelchair and mobility scooter accessible; lifts on all floors; assistance dogs welcome. Wheelchair-accessible restrooms with a low sink; changing place with a mobile hoist. Accessibility map at the information desk; skip-the-line form for visitors with an invisible disability. Guided tours at a reduced rate: € 30 instead of € 125.

Entrance cards

  • Museumkaart — Accepted (Free on presentation of the card; booking a start time is required.)
  • Vriendenloterij — Accepted (VriendenLoterij VIP-KAART: free.)
  • ICOM — Accepted (Free; no start time needed (ICOM/ICOMOS holders may enter without booking ahead).)
  • Amsterdamse Stadspas — Accepted (Free.)
  • I amsterdam City Card — Accepted (Free.)

Prices

Adults € 25; ages 18 and under free. Free with Museumkaart, I amsterdam City Card, ICOM, Amsterdamse Stadspas, Vereniging Rembrandt, KOG, VVAK and VriendenLoterij VIP-KAART. CJP/EYCA: 50% discount. Start time booking required for everyone; tickets online or at the door while stocks last.
