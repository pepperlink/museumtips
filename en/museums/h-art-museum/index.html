H'ART Museum (ex-Hermitage Amsterdam)

Back to all museums

Amsterdam

H'ART Museum presents exhibitions with art from famous museums. The museum is housed in the historic 17th-century Amstelhof building, located alongside the Amstel River in Amsterdam. The building features a unique inner courtyard and a Grand Café.

Museum website

Location

Amstel 51, Amsterdam (voormalig Amstelhof, aan de Amstel; auto/taxi-drop-off bij Nieuwe Keizersgracht 1)

Map unavailable; use the address above.

Opening hours

Daily 10:00–17:00; last entry at 16:30. 25 December and 1 January: 12:00–17:00. Closed on King's Day (27 April).

Getting there

Public transport

Tram 14: Waterlooplein stop. Metro 51, 53 and 54: Waterlooplein stop (exit Hortusplantsoen). Easily accessible by (OV-)bicycle; rent an OV-fiets at Amsterdam Central or Amsterdam Amstel station.

Parking

Parking garages a short walk away: Stadhuis/Muziektheater, Waterlooplein and Markenhoven. Cars, buses and taxis cannot stop in front of the museum; use the drop-off at Nieuwe Keizersgracht 1. Two accessible parking spaces, to be reserved via mail@hartmuseum.nl.

Accessibility

All rooms and facilities are wheelchair accessible; three accessible toilets and three public elevators. Manual wheelchairs and walkers can be borrowed free of charge (reservation recommended via mail@hartmuseum.nl). A companion may enter free of charge; guide dogs wearing harnesses are welcome. Accessible parking spaces can be reserved (see parking).

Prices

Adults € 12.50; youth (up to 17) free; CJP/Student € 7.50; Uitpas € 7.50. Chez Matisse (3 October 2026 – 14 February 2027): surcharge — adult + surcharge € 29.50; CJP/Student + surcharge € 18.50; Uitpas + surcharge € 18.50; Museumkaart + € 7; I amsterdam City Card/Go City + € 7; free for VriendenLoterij VIP-KAART, Stadspas, ICOM and H'ART Museum Members.
