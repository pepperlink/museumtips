Arnhem

Museum Arnhem is located on a high lateral moraine with a magnificent view of the Rhine, in one of the most beautifully situated museum buildings in the Netherlands. The collection is very broad, including contemporary art, magic realism and neorealism, fashion and jewellery. The freely accessible sculpture garden can be visited without a museum ticket.

Location

Getting there

Public transport

Train: about a 10-minute walk from Arnhem Central Station. Bus: lines 103 and 303 (direction Ede via Wageningen), stop Museum Arnhem (right in front of the museum). OV-bikes can be hired at Arnhem Central Station (less than 5 minutes by bike); bicycle shed next to the entrance.

Parking

Parking in front of the museum is not possible; dropping someone off is possible. Four parking spaces for disabled parking permit holders in front of the museum. Other visitors: car park Centraal Arnhem, Willemtunnel 1, 6811 KZ Arnhem (open 24/7, paid), about a 10-minute walk. Free: P+R GelreDome, Batavierenweg 25, 6841 HN Arnhem (bus to the city centre; not during events at the GelreDome).

Accessibility

Wheelchair accessible; suitable toilets and a lift available. Tuin Museum Arnhem is accessible by wheelchair, except for the stairs in the garden; the view can also be experienced in the new wing. Tip: use the café entrance (the slope at the main entrance can feel steep). Mobility scooter possible (max. 120 x 70 x 109 cm, in consultation). Wheelchair and walker available to borrow (reserve: receptie@museumarnhem.nl). One companion free; assistance dogs welcome (pets not allowed). Four free disabled parking spaces on Utrechtseweg.

Adults €15.00; kids 13-17 €5.00; kids 0-12 free; CJP card and student card €5.00; KBO members €13.00. Free: Museumkaart, Rembrandt card, ICOM card, VriendenLoterij VIP card, Gelrepas, ArtEZ students and Friends of Museum Arnhem. Buying a ticket online in advance is possible but not required; tickets are always available at the reception.
