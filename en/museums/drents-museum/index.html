Assen

The Drents Museum in Assen is known in the Netherlands and abroad for its major international exhibitions in the fields of archaeology, art and history. The permanent collection presentation Labyrinthia features highlights such as the Girl of Yde, the world's oldest boat and paintings by Van Gogh. Its subcollections include archaeology, Drenthe history, art from around 1900 and contemporary realism. DM De Buitenplaats in Eelde is part of the Drents Museum.

Opening hours

Tue–Sun 10 am–5 pm. Open on Mondays during Dutch school holidays and public holidays. Extra open (2026): Mon 16 & 23 Feb, Mon 6 Apr (Easter), Mon 25 May (Whit Monday), summer-holiday Mondays 4 Jul–30 Aug, Mon 12 & 19 Oct, Mon 21 & 28 Dec. Getting there

Public transport

Train/bus: about 8 minutes' walk from Assen Intercity and bus station. Bus (Qbuzz): stop Zuidhaege or Assen station.

Parking

Drents Museum parking garage (Torenlaan) and Stadhuis parking garage (Doevenkamp) nearby. Disabled parking spaces at several locations in Assen city centre.

Accessibility

Largely easily accessible for wheelchair users; most floors reachable by lift. A few places in the monumental section are difficult or impossible to reach. Free wheelchairs, rollators and folding stools to borrow (reservation recommended; reservations processed Mon–Thu). Wheelchairs/scooters wider than 110 cm are not allowed (narrowest passages ± 90 cm). Two wheelchair-accessible toilets; portable payment terminals. Special attention for visual and hearing impairments; Labyrinthia is not suitable for people who are easily overstimulated.
